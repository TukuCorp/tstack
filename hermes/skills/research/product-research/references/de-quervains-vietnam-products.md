# De Quervain's Tenosynovitis — Products & Vietnam Availability

*Research compiled June 30, 2026 for a user in HCMC, wife diagnosed postpartum (baby born May 8, 2026).*

## Medical context (AAOS/OrthoInfo)
- Associated with pregnancy & postpartum (symptoms within 4-6 weeks of birth)
- Pain aggravated by "lifting a child" (thumb-up position)
- Treatment hierarchy: activity modification → thumb spica splinting → icing → NSAIDs → steroid injection → surgery at 6+ weeks
- Wearing brace at night is critical (unconscious fist-clenching aggravates)
