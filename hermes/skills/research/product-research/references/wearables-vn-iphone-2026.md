## iOS compatibility per brand (the structural facts that matter)
- On iOS, essentially NO non-Apple watch can reply to SMS/WhatsApp notifications (Apple API
  restriction) — set expectations per brand; the real differentiator is companion-app polish
  + Apple Health sync depth, not reply capability.
- **Garmin**: Garmin Connect iOS + Apple Health sync (steps/HR/sleep/workouts). Notifications
  view-only — no replies on iOS (support.garmin.com FAQ Y7HkeoWBJp8xsQuLaE7sH8). FR165/Venu
  Sq 2 have no mic/speaker → no Bluetooth calls. Garmin Pay works on iPhone. Garmin Messenger
  (proprietary Garmin-to-Garmin) is the only watch-side messaging on iOS.
- **Amazfit**: Zepp app (App Store). Apple Health sync confirmed working (r/amazfit thread).
  No native iOS quick replies — Amazfit officially documents a Beeper workaround
  (us.amazfit.com "How to Reply to iOS Message Notifications"). Occasional notification
  drops reported (FB user groups).
- **Huawei**: officially "compatible with iOS and Android" (consumer.huawei.com spec page) via
  Huawei Health app; Bluetooth calling works. BUT iPhone notification delivery reportedly
  flaky (r/HuaweiWatchGT: "notifications are extremely unreliable"); no replies; partial
  Apple Health sync.
- **Xiaomi**: Mi Fitness app (iOS 14+ per mi.com specs). Notifications NON-INTERACTIVE on both
  Android and iOS — dismiss only (PCMag Watch S4 review). No replies. Bluetooth calling works.
- **Withings**: best-in-class iOS — Health Mate + deep Apple Health sync, FDA-cleared ECG works
  on iPhone; no replies (no keyboard, by design).

## Battery claims vs reality (rough rule)
Garmin/Amazfit/Huawei/Xiaomi claims ≈ 1.3–1.5× real-world with always-on display; Withings
30-day claims hold (~3–4 weeks). Specifics: FR165 11d claim; Active 2 10d; GT5 14d max/9d
typical (46mm); Venu Sq 2 11d; Vivomove Trend only 4–5d (hybrid trade-off); Watch S4 15d;
ScanWatch 2 30d (→~35d after 2025 update).
