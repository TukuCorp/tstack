## Wirecutter — "The Best Wired Earbuds" (updated Mar 24, 2026)
URL: https://www.nytimes.com/wirecutter/reviews/the-best-200-in-ear-headphones/
(old slug /reviews/best-wired-earbuds/ 404s; site-search if slug changes again)

Picks (all above $50 except noted):
- Top pick (neutral): Linsoul Tin HiFi T4 — $89, MMCX, aluminum/stainless build, no mic.
  "balanced sound... bass notes provide support without being overpowering."
- Top pick (bright): FiiO FD3 Pro — $160, semi-open 12mm DD, 11 tip sets, swappable
  plugs (3.5/2.5/4.4mm). Highs "strategically intensified"; fatiguing for some.
- Budget pick: Linsoul Tin HiFi T3 Plus — $59. "sound fantastic for the price...
  delicate high notes were clearly articulated, bass notes had presence without
  sounding muddy, and vocals shone without being harsh." Two-pin cable, no mic/case.
- Upgrade: FiiO FH5s — $255, 3-way hybrid, tuning switches.

Under-$50 section (the money quotes):
- Moondrop Quarks (~$15): "a delightfully uncomplicated pair of wired earbuds that
  sounds better than its low price would suggest... more balanced bass response than
  anything else we've heard under $20." Has 3-button remote + clear analog mic.
- Sony IER-EX15C USB-C (~$20-25): warm sound, solid bass, rare 3-button remote/mic,
  3 tip sizes. Recommended over Sennheiser CX 80U.

REJECTED by Wirecutter 2026 update (hyped models they dismissed):
- Moondrop Chu II: "emphasized mids and produced harsh, twangy highs... ear tips and
  the cable felt stiff."
- 7Hz x Crinacle Zero:2 ($25): "build quality felt cheaper... rolled-off low end and
  overly intense, inauthentic [treble]."
- Tripowin x OdiBi Vivace (sub-$40): U-shaped, "jagged intensity in the highs."
- Sennheiser CX 80U, Sennheiser IE 200 (bass failed to represent low end).

- Parse pattern: strip tags, rows look like

  Moondrop SSR/SSP $40 C, Tin HiFi T2 Pro $50 C (bright neutral), BLON BL05 $43 C,

- Above $50 context: Truthear Hexa $80 B+ ★★★ (neutral, "redefines the price bracket"),
