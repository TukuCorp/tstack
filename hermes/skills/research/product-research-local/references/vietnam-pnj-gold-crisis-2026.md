### Structural issues
- **SJC monopoly** on gold bar production creates artificial premium
- **SBV import controls** restrict supply, widening domestic-international gap
- **Household hoarding**: Vietnamese households hold 100+ tons of gold outside banking system
- **Smuggling arbitrage**: Gold smuggled from Cambodia to exploit the price gap
- **SBV intervention**: Periodic auctions and stabilization programs (2025+) with limited success

### The recovery pattern (from Chow Tai Fook precedent)

```
Scandal breaks → Stock crashes 40-60% → 
Insider arrests → Regulatory probes → 
Company hires independent auditors → 
New compliance framework → 
Gradual trust recovery (12-18 months) → 
Industry consolidation
```

PNJ is in **Phase 2** (crash + arrests) as of Jul 2026.
